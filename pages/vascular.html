Venous Malformations

Usually slow-flow abnormalities of veins. They may appear blue or purple and can be compressible, painful, or deep in soft tissue.

Open venous conditions

Lymphatic Malformations

Abnormally formed lymphatic channels. Lesions may be macrocystic, microcystic, or mixed and can occur in many body regions.

Open lymphatic conditions

Capillary Malformations

Slow-flow lesions of small vessels in the skin, including port-wine birthmarks and some syndromic presentations.

Open capillary conditions

Arteriovenous Malformations

Fast-flow connections between arteries and veins that bypass the capillary bed. Symptoms and risks depend on location.

Some conditions involve more than one vessel type. The site’s Klippel-Trenaunay Syndrome page is one example.
