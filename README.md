# The Veiniverse (Beyond the Blueprint)

An educational platform that raises awareness and understanding of genetic diseases and vascular malformations.

## What it does
- Brings together medical research, 3D anatomical models, and treatment resources to help patients and caregivers understand complex conditions
- Offers accessible resources that connect people with specialists, educational materials, and community support

## Built with
UI/UX design, Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
